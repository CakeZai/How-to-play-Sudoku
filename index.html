How to play Sudoku.

Welcome to how to play Sudoku 101

Sudoku is a logic-based puzzle played on a 9×9 grid divided into nine 3×3 boxes. The goal is simple: fill every empty cell with digits from 1 to 9 so that no number repeats within any row, column, or 3×3 box. No math or guessing required-just process of elimination.

I picked up Sudoku toward the end of high school. While I don't play daily anymore, it stuck with me enough that during last year's break, I managed to hit 10th place on the leaderboard.

Why I play?

Most video games either make me rage-quit or end up sucking away hours without much reward (I'd rather just watch let's plays). Sudoku is different. After solving my very first board to flex on my siblings, I was hooked. It's quiet, satisfying, and gives your brain a real workout without the frustration.

How to Get Started

You don't need anything fancy:

  • Go digital: Download an app like Classic Sudoku Puzzle (the dark mode makes late-night solving way easier on the eyes).
  • Go physical: Grab a cheap Sudoku puzzle book at any grocery store or bookstore if you prefer pencil and paper.

Where to Learn More?

Advanced techniques can get difficult when you're aiming for speed, but your very first puzzle only takes basic logic. There are a lot of Youtube videos for beginner walk throughs like "Sudoku row scanning" or "pencil marks" to pick up easy speed tricks.
